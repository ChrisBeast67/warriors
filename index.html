The Five Clans

Four Clans live in the forest. The fifth is lost — but not forgotten.

⚡

ThunderClan

Proud, bold and certain they are the best of the Clans. Their territory is deep in the forest, and their leader sits in a high oak watching over it.

🌑

ShadowClan

Mysterious, quiet and loyal to their own. They hunt in the pine forest, and their warriors keep their distance from the daylight.

🌊

RiverClan

Home of the fastest swimmers and the best fishers. Their territory runs along the Clearwater, where the current is strong and the sun never quite sets.

☁️

WindClan

They live up on the windy moorland, where the air is thin and the grass bends flat. WindClan cats are tough, free and fiercely independent.

⭐

SkyClan

The lost fifth Clan. Their ancestors left the forest to find a new home beyond the mountains — and were never seen again. Until they came back.

Why You'll Love It

🔥 Real stakes

Every battle matters. Leaders die, Clans split, and the Code gets rewritten by the people who survived it. The consequences actually land.

🗣️ A whole new language

Seasons are newleaf, greenleaf, leaf-fall and leaf-bare. Months are moons. Cars are monsters. Humans are twolegs. It all clicks after two chapters.

🌙 StarClan is watching

Dead cats live on in the stars, and their spirits can guide, warn or haunt. The afterlife is part of the story, not an epilogue.

🩹 Medicine cats matter

Healers, herbalists and detectives. Often the most important cat in the story is the one nobody thought to look at.

⚖️ The Code bends

The Warrior Code is the moral spine of the series — and one whole arc is about what happens when it turns out to be broken.

🌍 One big timeline

From the first Clans meeting in Dawn of the Clans to the newest arc, every book lands in one continuous history you can trace.

About Erin Hunter

Warriors is written under a shared pen name. The concept and original storyline were created by Victoria Holmes, and the first novels were written by Kate Cary and Cherith Baldry. The Erin Hunter team has changed and expanded many times since — including Tui T. Sutherland, who also wrote the spin-off series Wings of Fire.

The series has been translated into 38 languages and sold over 90 million copies worldwide. A film was planned and later cancelled; an animated series began development in 2026 and is set to air in 2028.
