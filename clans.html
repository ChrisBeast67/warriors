Five Clans, one forest

The Clans

Each Clan adapted to its own terrain, its own prey, its own way of seeing the world.
That is why they are so different — and why they keep clashing.

The Five

Four Clans in the forest territories, plus the lost fifth.

⚡ ThunderClan

Deep in the forest, where the trees are thick and the prey is plentiful. ThunderClan believes it is the best of the Clans and says so loudly. Their leader watches from a high oak, and their camp is defended by a gorse thicket and a stream.

Proud · Bold · LoyalForest territory

Known for: strength in numbers, and for the Lightning Rod — the oak where the Clans gather for the grand Clan meetings.

🌑 ShadowClan

In the pine forest at the edge of the Clans' land, where the trees grow close and the shadows are deep. ShadowClan cats are quiet, watchful and careful — and they keep to themselves. Their leader's den sits on a rocky ledge near a narrow pass.

Quiet · Watchful · IndependentPine forest territory

Known for: the Two Paths, and for cats who will cross enemy territory in the dead of night.

🌊 RiverClan

Along the Clearwater, where the current runs fast and the fish are plentiful. RiverClan cats are strong swimmers and superb hunters of the water. Their territory is a stretch of riverbank and the caves above it.

Strong · Athletic · PracticalRiver territory

Known for: the scent of the river, a loud voice, and a leader's den above the racing water.

☁️ WindClan

Out on the open moorland, where the grass grows short and the wind never stops. WindClan cats are tough and free-spirited — they hunt the moor and sleep under the stars with nothing but the sky above them.

Free · Tough · HardworkingMoorland territory

Known for: highstones to shelter in, and for a stubbornness that outlasts everyone else's.

⭐ SkyClan — the lost fifth Clan

SkyClan did not choose exile. Long ago, when they were still part of the forest Clans, the other four drove them out because they wanted to live in the trees with their paws on the ground rather than up in the branches. SkyClan crossed the mountains to find a new home — and vanished.

Migratory · Resilient · LongingBeyond the mountains

Known for: living in the tree tops, for the long walk that nearly ended them, and for the arc where they came back — and found the lake taken.

Outside the Clans

Not every cat in the forest belongs to a Clan.

🐾 The Tribe of Rushing Water

A colony of cats living beside a rushing waterfall in the mountains, hunting fish and avoiding the Clans entirely. They appear in The New Prophecy, and their story leads right back to the founding of the Clans in Dawn of the Clans.

⚫ Rogue Clans

Cats who reject the Clans' way of life and hunt alone, or in small loose groups. Some rogues are desperate predators; some are just cats who never found a Clan.

👻 The Spirit Clans

After Omen of the Stars, evil spirit cats form their own Clans in the afterlife and train living cats from their dreams. StarClan descends from the sky to fight them.

🏠 Kittypets

Housecats that live with twolegs. Clan cats find them strange and pitiful — and mostly avoid them.

🩸 The BloodClan

A gang of city cats led by Scourge that lives in a human town. They attack the Clans in The Prophecies Begin — and Scourge kills Tigerstar.

🐺 The Clans of the Dark Forest

The Place Where the Shadows Hide — a hostile, twisted version of the forest beyond the Clans' territory, where a very different set of cats rules.

Names & Ranks

Every cat's name has two parts, and the second part changes as they climb.

How a name works

A cat's name is a prefix plus a suffix. The prefix is usually a colour or something from the natural world — Fir, Oak, Pool, Thorn, Silver. The suffix shows rank:

  • -kit — a newborn, before naming
  • -paw — a young cat still in the nursery
  • a unique suffix — given when a cat becomes a warrior
  • -star — given when a cat becomes leader of a Clan
  • -claw — given to medicine cats

So Firestar was once Firpaw, then Fireheart, then Firestar.

Medicine cats

Every Clan has medicine cats: healers who know herbs, read omens and treat the sick and injured. Their suffix ends in -claw — Yellowfang, Spottedleaf, Littlepool.

They also keep the Clans' most important records, because medicine cats often live longer than any warrior. A lot of what we know about the oldest days comes from them.

StarClan grants leaders extra lives — most famously nine — and medicine cats often hold them in trust for a leader who needs one.

The Religions

🌟 StarClan

The spirits of dead cats, living in the sky. They watch over the Clans, send omens, and — when the Clans are in real danger — can physically descend to help. StarClan is the Clans' religion, their history and their conscience.

🌑 The Dark Forest

A place beyond the Two Paths where the sun never reaches. The Cats of the Dark Forest believe survival is the only law, and that the Clans' Code is a luxury that gets cats killed. Some of the Clans' most unsettling arcs come from here.

🌿 The Silent Forest / Spirit Warriors

In the later arcs, some cats believe the spirit cats of the Dark Forest are the true power, and rally under the banner of the Spirit Warriors. It is a frightening arc because it comes from inside the Clans.
