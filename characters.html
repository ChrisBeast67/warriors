Everyone who matters

Characters

Heroes, villains, medicine cats and the ones in between — the cats that made the series.

ThunderClan

Proud, brave and — for most of the series — the heart of the story.

⚡ Firestar

The main protagonist of The Prophecies Begin. Born a house cat named Rusty, he is invited to join ThunderClan after venturing into their forest territory. He exposes Tigerclaw, unites the Clans against Scourge, and dies defeating Tigerstar's spirit in The Last Hope.

Leader of ThunderClan

⭐ Bluestar

Leader of ThunderClan before Firestar. She was responsible for allowing Firestar to join the Clan. One of the most loved characters in the whole series — her relationship with Yellowfang is at the heart of it.

Leader of ThunderClan

🐾 Graystripe

Firestar's best friend. He was in love with Silverstream of RiverClan, with whom he had two kits, Feathertail and Stormfur. He was ThunderClan's deputy for a time, but after he was captured by humans his position was revoked. He died in A Light in the Mist.

Warrior of ThunderClan

🩺 Yellowfang

ThunderClan's medicine cat after Spottedleaf. Former ShadowClan medicine cat, and mother of Brokenstar. Before becoming a medicine cat she was a warrior, forced to resign because she could feel the pain of other cats.

Medicine cat

🌿 Spottedleaf

Medicine cat of ThunderClan when Firestar first joins. She is murdered in the very first book, but she keeps guiding Firestar from StarClan. Her spirit is killed in the Great Battle in The Last Hope.

Medicine cat

🌿 Leafpool

Medicine cat of ThunderClan after Cinderpelt, and mother to Hollyleaf, Jayfeather and Lionblaze. Daughter of Firestar — which makes the Power of Three prophecy a family matter.

Medicine cat

🌿 Jayfeather

ThunderClan's medicine cat after Leafpool. He is blind, and can enter the dreams of other cats. He can also sense others' feelings and thoughts. The gentlest of the three Power of Three cats — and the one who pays for it.

Medicine cat

⚡ Hollyleaf

Warrior of ThunderClan, and sister of Jayfeather and Lionblaze. She runs away from the Clans after realising she has no powers, and later murders Ashfur. She eventually returns to ThunderClan.

Warrior of ThunderClan

⚡ Lionblaze

One of the three Power of Three cats. Fierce, reckless and fiercely loyal — Lionblaze fights a war in the Dark Forest and comes back changed, learning that the Code does not have to be the only way to live.

Warrior of ThunderClan

🦅 Dovewing

Warrior of ThunderClan who was given the ability to hear far beyond what a normal cat could. She later left ThunderClan to live in ShadowClan with her mate, the second Tigerstar. Mother of Shadowsight and Lightleap.

Warrior of ThunderClan

🌿 Ivypool

Deputy of ThunderClan who served as a spy in the Dark Forest. Sister to Dovewing, and mother to Bristlefrost. Caught between two worlds — and it goes badly wrong.

Deputy of ThunderClan

❄️ Bristlefrost

Warrior of ThunderClan, and a hero of The Broken Code. She played a major role in defeating Ashfur — and ultimately gave her life to do it, in such a way that her spirit was killed too.

Warrior of ThunderClan

🌙 Nightheart

Warrior of ThunderClan. He is restless and uncomfortable with being related to Firestar, feeling unfairly pressured to live up to the great leader. Mate to Sunbeam.

Warrior of ThunderClan

🌅 Moonglow

Former ThunderClan cat, and one of the main characters of the ninth arc, Changing Skies. She is haunted by the spirit of her dead sister, Brightmorning, who refuses to move on to StarClan.

Protagonist, The Elders' Quest

🌿 Twigbranch

Warrior of ThunderClan, former warrior of SkyClan. Sister of Violetshine. She carries the scars of exile — and of SkyClan coming home.

Warrior of ThunderClan

RiverClan

💧 Mistystar

Daughter of Bluestar, and leader of RiverClan after Leopardstar. She proves that leadership is not only about being the strongest fighter in the room.

Leader of RiverClan

💧 Leopardstar

Leader of RiverClan before Mistystar, and one of the most controversial figures in the series — loyal to his own and ruthless to everyone else.

Leader of RiverClan

🌊 Crookedstar

Leader of RiverClan before Leopardstar. Haunted in his youth by Mapleshade, the spirit of the Dark Forest. Father of Silverstream.

Leader of RiverClan

🌊 Silverstream

RiverClan warrior, daughter of Crookedstar and mate of Graystripe. She died while giving birth to her kits, Feathertail and Stormfur.

Warrior of RiverClan

🐾 Feathertail

Chosen RiverClan cat for the journey to find the lake territories. She saved the Tribe of Rushing Water from a mountain lion called Sharptooth — and died doing it, choosing to stay in the Tribe's version of StarClan.

Chosen cat

🐾 Stormfur

Son of Graystripe and Silverstream, brother of Feathertail, and a RiverClan warrior. Mate to Brook Where Small Fish Swim, and cave-guard of the Tribe of Rushing Water.

Warrior of RiverClan

🌿 Mothwing

Medicine cat of RiverClan. She didn't believe in StarClan for a very long time — then admitted it exists, while still questioning the idea of being guided by spirits.

Medicine cat

❄️ Icestar

Current leader of RiverClan. Raised by Dark Forest cats (the Kin) before escaping into the Clans — which makes her leadership a constant negotiation between who she was taught to be and who she chose to be.

Leader of RiverClan

💧 Splashtail

Became the false leader of RiverClan for a time through manipulative plots and schemes. She eventually went to the Dark Forest.

False leader

WindClan

☁️ Tallstar

Leader of WindClan when Firestar first joins ThunderClan. He died of old age in Starlight, which triggered a leadership struggle between his deputy Mudclaw and Onewhisker, whom he named as deputy just before he died.

Leader of WindClan

☁️ Onestar

Leader of WindClan after Tallstar. He had an illegitimate son named Darktail, and rejected him when he was young. He ultimately died fighting him.

Leader of WindClan

🕊️ Crowfeather

Deputy of WindClan under Harestar, and the chosen WindClan cat for the journey to find the lake territories. He mated with Leafpool, and was the father of Hollyleaf, Lionblaze and Jayfeather.

Chosen cat

🕊️ Breezepelt

Son of Crowfeather and Nightcloud — and revealed in Long Shadows to be half-brother to Hollyleaf, Jayfeather and Lionblaze. He trained in the Dark Forest, angry at his father's rejection of him, before eventually reconciling.

Warrior of WindClan

🌙 Nightcloud

Mother of Breezepelt and former mate of Crowfeather. The pair separate after Sunrise reveals Crowfeather's other family, argue bitterly — and eventually reconcile in Crowfeather's Trial.

Former mate

ShadowClan

🌑 Tigerstar (the first)

The villain of the first arc. Tigerclaw, betrayed and exiled, joins ShadowClan and becomes its leader, Tigerstar. He brings the leader of a group of city cats, Scourge, to the forest. His greed starts a war.

Leader of ShadowClan

🌑 Tigerstar (the second)

Leader of ShadowClan after Rowanclaw, named after his grandfather. His mate is Dovewing, and his son is Shadowsight — so this Tigerstar's story ends much more gently than the first one's.

Leader of ShadowClan

🌑 Rowanclaw

Leader of ShadowClan after Blackstar. He stepped down in cowardice after failing to respond to Darktail's group of strays appropriately.

Leader of ShadowClan

🌑 Brokenstar

Leader of ShadowClan when Firestar comes to the forest. He killed kits by sending them into battle before they were strong enough to defend themselves. His soul was sent to the Dark Forest when he died.

Leader of ShadowClan

🌑 Blackstar

Deputy of ShadowClan under Brokenstar and the first Tigerstar, and later leader. He served as a bruiser for both — before eventually being redeemed in a StarClan-held trial.

Leader of ShadowClan

🌿 Shadowsight

Medicine cat of ShadowClan. He played a major role in defeating Ashfur after being manipulated by him. He has no connection to StarClan.

Medicine cat

🌿 Tawnypelt

Former apprentice of ThunderClan who joined ShadowClan when her father, the first Tigerstar, became its leader. She mated with Rowanclaw and had several kits, including the second Tigerstar and Flametail.

Warrior of ShadowClan

⚔️ Needletail

Former apprentice of ShadowClan. She joined Darktail's group of strays for a time — before eventually giving her life to save Violetshine.

Former apprentice

🐾 Berryheart

Former warrior of ShadowClan, and later a false RiverClan deputy. Splashtail's accomplice, and Sunbeam's mother. She went to the Dark Forest after she died.

Former warrior

🌑 Darktail

Onestar's illegitimate and bitter son. Leader of a group of cats who attacked the Clans and tried to disband them — the central villain of A Vision of Shadows.

Leader of the strays

SkyClan

⭐ Leafstar

Leader of SkyClan in Firestar's Quest, when the Clans are still in the forest. A proud leader of a proud Clan — before the exile that changes everything.

Leader of SkyClan

⭐ Tree

SkyClan mediator, formerly of the Sisters. He mates with Violetshine, with whom he has Rootspring. He can see and talk to spirits.

Mediator

⭐ Violetshine

SkyClan warrior who lived in ShadowClan and in Darktail's group of strays for a time. One of the most quietly devastating characters in the series.

Warrior of SkyClan

⭐ Rootspring

SkyClan warrior who played a major part in defeating Ashfur. Like his father, Tree, he can see and talk to spirits. He fell in love with Bristlefrost.

Warrior of SkyClan

⭐ Starlingflight

SkyClan warrior. He worries over an ominous prophecy he overhears on a journey to the Tribe, then begins telling lies to advance himself within the Clan. His outbursts against authority figures foster division in SkyClan.

Warrior of SkyClan

⭐ Cloudstar

Leader of the ancient SkyClan, the Clans as they existed before the forest was their home. His decisions echo all the way into the modern era.

Leader of ancient SkyClan

Outside the Clans

💀 Scourge

Formerly named Tiny. After being attacked by the first Tigerstar as a kit, Scourge grew to resent the Clans and came to lead the cats of a human town as a dictator. He kills Tigerstar — and Firestar kills him.

Leader of the town cats

🌑 Mapleshade

Ancient spirit she-cat who haunts the dreams of other cats, particularly Crookedstar and the first Tigerstar. She resides in the Dark Forest, and she is one of the series' best villains for it.

Spirit of the Dark Forest

🌑 Sol

Formerly a house cat named Harry. After a bad experience with SkyClan at their gorge camp, Sol decided to find the lake Clans and try to disband them. A villain without a single warrior's instinct.

Schemer

🌑 Ashfur

Former warrior of ThunderClan. He fell in love with Squirrelstar, and was heartbroken when she chose Brambleclaw. After he died, he used his time in StarClan to plan his takeover of the living Clans — and nearly succeeded.

Main villain, The Broken Code

🐦 Ravenpaw

Former ThunderClan apprentice who came to live on a farm with Barley after fleeing in fear of being killed by the first Tigerstar, having witnessed his crimes. The protagonist of the Ravenpaw's Path manga trilogy.

Protagonist, Ravenpaw's Path

🦡 Midnight

Badger who has existed since the dawn of time. She tells the journeying cats of The New Prophecy that their forest home is about to be destroyed — and how to find a new one. Unlike other badgers, she can speak to cats.

Badger, New Prophecy

The Ancient Ones

Dawn of the Clans — the cats who built everything that came after.

☀️ Clear Sky

First leader of ThunderClan, later Thunderstar. Son of Gray Wing.

🌗 Gray Wing

Known as "Graywing the Wise" to modern Clan cats. He majorly influenced the creation of the modern Clans.

🌙 Half Moon

Also known as Stoneteller and Teller of the Pointed Stones. First leader of what would become the Tribe of Rushing Water.

🕊️ Moth Flight

First medicine cat and member of WindClan. Daughter of Wind Runner.

⚡ Thunder

First leader of ThunderClan, later Thunderstar. Son of Clear Sky.

🌊 River Ripple

First leader of RiverClan, later Riverstar. Previously a stray cat who lived in a park far away.

☁️ Wind Runner

First leader of WindClan, later Windstar. Mother of Moth Flight.

👁️ One Eye

Stray who caused trouble for the Clans during their inception. Founder of the Dark Forest.
